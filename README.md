# The Oklahoma Trail

The game is very self-explanatory. You play as a cattle driver on the Chisholm Trail, hoping to earn some money by selling your cattle. Over 6 weeks, you will face many difficulties, and the party depends on you to make decisions: choose wisely.

To start, click anywhere. Then, using the text on the screen, decide what role you want to play or click “4” for more information. To select anything, make sure to press the key that the game tells you to click. After selecting, there is some introductory text. To move to the next text box, click “z.” If pressing “z” doesn’t do anything, it is because you haven’t gone far enough. You will need to wait until the number of miles traveled is high enough for the next week. Hang tight! Eventually, you will get to week 1 where you have 2 options. Follow the text on the screen and click either “1” or “2” to pick your option. Continue reading text and select “1” or “2” for options until you reach the end of the game.

On repeated playthroughs, hitting the X key will allow you to jump forward in miles, in order to skip forward to more action. WARNING: This game will most likely not be won in the first attempt. Be resilient and learn from your mistakes!
